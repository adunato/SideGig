# Skillshare

- **Channel class:** Knowledge and Technical-Content Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://www.skillshare.com/

## Description

A subscription learning marketplace where teachers publish classes for members. Creators are compensated through Skillshare’s teacher payment model while gaining access to an established audience seeking creative, business and technical learning content.
