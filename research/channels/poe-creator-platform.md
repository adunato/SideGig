# Poe Creator Platform

- **Channel class:** AI Model, Agent and Inference Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://poe.com/creators
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

Poe’s creator ecosystem for publishing AI bots to Poe users. Creators can build prompt bots or connect their own model/API endpoints and monetise usage through per-message pricing and Poe’s creator revenue-sharing infrastructure.
