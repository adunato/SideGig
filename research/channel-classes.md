# Channel Classes

## Status

Step 3 complete. Each channel class now includes the research sources to be used for channel discovery in Step 4.

This document defines the broad classes of sales and distribution channels to be investigated. Classes are based primarily on how a product is distributed, purchased and consumed rather than on specific product ideas or individual platforms.

Research sources are **discovery sources**, not evidence that a channel is commercially attractive. They are used to generate candidate channels. Candidate channels must subsequently be verified from first-party sources and assessed separately against the [Channel Assessment Framework](channel-assessment-framework.md).
