# API.market

- **Channel class:** API and Microservice Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://api.market/
- **Assessment status:** Assessed
- **Assessment date:** 2026-08-31

## Description

API.market is a developer-facing marketplace for commercial APIs and AI models. Providers connect their existing HTTP APIs, configure subscription or usage-based pricing, and sell through API.market's managed gateway, which provides authentication, metering, billing, documentation, monitoring, payouts and automatically generated MCP access for agent clients.

## Channel Assessment

Assessment framework: [../channel-assessment-framework.md](../channel-assessment-framework.md)

A higher score always means a more attractive channel for this project.

| Metric | Score (1–5) | Confidence | Evidence and rationale |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Paying demand | **3** | Medium | API.market currently claims **500+ APIs and AI models, 13,000+ developers and 6M+ monthly requests**. It supports paid subscriptions, pay-per-use plans and global seller payouts, and its public marketplace labels products as best-selling. These signals establish active usage and a functioning paid mechanism, but current GMV, number of paying developers and provider payout totals are not public; the request count may include free usage. |
| Opportunity density | **3** | Low | The catalogue is relatively small at 500+ products across 80+ providers, while the platform reports 6M+ monthly requests. That is directionally encouraging compared with much larger catalogues, but request and revenue concentration are unknown and the marketplace includes substantial inventories from a handful of providers. The available proxies support a plausible opportunity rather than a proven favourable distribution. |
| New-entrant attainability | **3** | Low | Listing is free, seller onboarding is self-service, and API.market says most providers can go live the same day. It also provides recommendation and targeted-marketing mechanisms. However, no recent independent-provider cohort or earnings evidence was found showing what a newly listed API typically achieves, so the score remains provisional despite low formal entry barriers. |
| Revenue potential | **4** | Medium | Providers keep **80% of sales**, with no listing or monthly marketplace fee, and the current request volume is large enough that the project's £2,000–£5,000 annual target is plausible for a useful API. The founder's earlier API business reached about $1,500/month on RapidAPI and his research found ten API providers collectively earning more than $50,000/month, demonstrating the broader product model, but those figures are historical and predate API.market itself. Current API.market seller-income distribution is not public. |
| Competitive pressure | **4** | Medium | With roughly 500 products and 80 providers, the channel is far less saturated than Zyla or RapidAPI. API.market also claims targeted recommendations and customer matchmaking. Competition still exists in popular AI/data categories and provider concentration may be meaningful, but the current catalogue size leaves more room for differentiated products than the largest open hubs. |
| Production leverage | **5** | High | The channel is strongly aligned with fast independent software production. Any suitable HTTP API can be packaged as a product, and API.market automatically handles gateway access, keys, quotas, billing, generated documentation and MCP exposure. This allows engineering and AI-assisted development to focus on the underlying differentiated capability rather than commerce plumbing. |
| Operating burden | **3** | High | API.market removes gateway, metering, billing, documentation and monitoring work, but the provider still runs the underlying endpoint. Its seller documentation explicitly says the seller keeps running their own API while the marketplace handles the layers between endpoint and customer. A successful product therefore retains live-service reliability and support obligations, making it less pausable than a downloadable digital product. |

### Supporting assessments

- **Trend:** Growing
- **Evidence confidence:** Medium

API.market appears to be an active, smaller challenger marketplace. It has continued shipping developer/agent features, including MCP integration, and currently reports 500+ APIs, 80+ providers and millions of monthly requests. The lack of public GMV or payout distribution limits confidence in its commercial attractiveness for a typical new provider.
