# Research Execution

Use this skill for work under the SideGig research methodology.

## Inputs

Read:

- `requirements.md`;
- `research/methodology.md`;
- the relevant channel class, channel, opportunity-area and existing evidence artifacts;
- the applicable template under `research/templates/`.

Identify the exact methodology phase, step or gateway before editing.

## Rules

- Keep discovery and assessment separate where the methodology requires separate passes.
- Prefer direct, current and first-party evidence; use proxies only where direct evidence is unavailable.
- Distinguish verified evidence, analytical inference, assumptions, limitations and confidence.
- Do not treat listings, anecdotes or isolated success stories as sufficient proof of demand.
- Maintain the comparison set and relevant indexes when adding a channel or opportunity area.
- Use the canonical templates and existing artifact structure rather than inventing parallel formats.
- Preserve unresolved uncertainty instead of manufacturing precision.
- Do not skip a methodology gateway merely because a candidate looks attractive.

For community research, distinguish recurring evidence from isolated reports and promotional claims.

## Repository changes

Use `sidegig-change` for the Git/PR workflow.

Use `validate-sidegig` before hand-off. Run research report/dashboard generation when the changed research state materially affects those derived views.

## Completion contract

Report the methodology phase/step, artifacts changed, evidence added or updated, material inferences/uncertainties, validation performed, and the next methodology step or gateway.
