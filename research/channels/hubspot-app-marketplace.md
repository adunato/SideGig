# HubSpot App Marketplace

- **Channel class:** Platform App, Plugin and Extension Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://ecosystem.hubspot.com/marketplace/apps
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

HubSpot’s marketplace for integrations and applications serving CRM, marketing, sales and service users. Developers can distribute specialised B2B software into HubSpot’s installed customer base, with monetisation generally occurring through the connected application.
