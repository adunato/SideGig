# Etsy

- **Channel class:** General Digital-Product Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://www.etsy.com/
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

A very large consumer marketplace that supports instant-download and made-to-order digital products alongside physical goods. Digital sellers can access strong built-in buyer traffic, although the marketplace is heavily oriented toward design, craft and consumer products.
