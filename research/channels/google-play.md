# Google Play

- **Channel class:** Standalone Application Stores
- **Channel URL:** https://play.google.com/store/apps
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

Google’s primary application marketplace for Android. Developers can publish standalone apps and games, charge for downloads, offer in-app purchases and subscriptions, and reach the global Android user base through Google Play distribution.
