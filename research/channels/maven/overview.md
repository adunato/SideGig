# Maven

- **Channel class:** Knowledge and Technical-Content Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://maven.com/
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

A marketplace and delivery platform for expert-led courses, particularly professional and technical education. Instructors can sell structured learning products to Maven’s audience, although many offerings use cohort-based or scheduled delivery.
