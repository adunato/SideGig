# Channel Classes

## Status

Step 2 complete. Research sources will be added under each class during Step 3.

This document defines the broad classes of sales and distribution channels to be investigated. Classes are based primarily on how a product is distributed, purchased and consumed rather than on specific product ideas or individual platforms.

---

## Standalone Application Stores

### Description

Stores through which customers discover, purchase or subscribe to standalone applications for mobile, desktop or other end-user operating environments. Products are complete applications rather than extensions to another commercial product, and monetisation may include paid downloads, in-app purchases or subscriptions.

## Game Distribution Stores

### Description

Stores and marketplaces through which developers distribute complete games directly to players. These channels typically provide discovery, payment, delivery, updates and community or review mechanisms, with monetisation ranging from one-off purchases to downloadable content and in-game transactions.

## Platform App, Plugin and Extension Marketplaces

### Description

Marketplaces attached to an established software platform where third-party developers sell or distribute functionality that extends the host product. Products include apps, plugins, browser extensions, IDE extensions, ecommerce add-ons, productivity integrations and other platform-specific software.

## API and Microservice Marketplaces

### Description

Channels where customers discover and purchase access to hosted APIs or narrowly scoped software capabilities. The product is normally consumed programmatically and monetised through subscriptions, usage-based pricing, transaction volumes or tiered access rather than through a traditional user-facing application.

## Cloud Software Marketplaces

### Description

Cloud-provider and enterprise technology marketplaces through which customers procure third-party software that runs on, integrates with or is billed through a cloud ecosystem. Products may include SaaS applications, containers, virtual-machine images, infrastructure software and other deployable services, commonly with subscription or usage-based pricing.

## Automation and Integration Marketplaces

### Description

Channels centred on reusable automations, workflows, connectors, integrations and actions that link existing software products or automate business processes. Products may be executable integrations, packaged workflows or reusable automation components sold to users who already operate within a particular automation ecosystem.

Channels where customers discover, use or pay for AI models, specialised agents, inference endpoints, agent tools, MCP servers or other reusable AI capabilities. Monetisation can be based on usage, subscriptions, revenue sharing or paid access to a hosted capability.

## Data Marketplaces

### Description

Marketplaces through which datasets, data feeds, derived data products or continuously updated information services are licensed or sold. Customers are typically businesses, developers, analysts or AI/data teams, with monetisation commonly based on subscriptions, usage, data volume or licence terms.

### Description

Marketplaces selling reusable technical products to software developers, including source-code packages, libraries, scripts, UI components, boilerplates, developer utilities and packaged tooling. The buyer generally integrates the purchased product into their own software or development workflow.

### Description

Marketplaces embedded in or serving website builders, productivity tools, ecommerce platforms and no-code environments where customers buy reusable templates, themes, workflows, dashboards or preconfigured systems. Marketplaces licensing reusable digital assets such as 3D models, graphics, icons, audio, video, fonts and other production media. Buyers purchase assets for incorporation into their own products, content or commercial work rather than consuming them as standalone applications.

## General Digital-Product Marketplaces

### Description

Broad marketplaces where independent sellers can offer downloadable or digitally delivered products without being tied to a particular software ecosystem. Products may include software, files, guides, templates, specialised content and other finished digital goods, with the marketplace providing varying degrees of discovery, checkout and fulfilment.

## Software Deal and Lifetime-Licence Marketplaces

### Description

Marketplaces that distribute standalone software or SaaS products through time-limited promotions, discounted subscriptions, lifetime licences or similar deal structures. These channels aggregate buyers actively looking for new software and can provide concentrated distribution in exchange for unusual pricing or commercial terms.

## Knowledge and Technical-Content Marketplaces

### Description

Marketplaces where customers purchase finished educational or reference products such as technical courses, books, guides, tutorials or structured learning material. The channel provides an existing audience seeking knowledge products rather than requiring the seller to build a standalone publishing audience from scratch.

## Direct Software and Digital-Product Storefronts

### Description

Platforms that provide checkout, subscriptions, licensing, tax handling, digital delivery or merchant-of-record services while leaving most customer acquisition to the seller. These channels enable direct sale of software, hosted services and digital products without requiring the seller to operate their own commerce infrastructure, but usually provide less built-in demand than a marketplace.
