# Shopify Theme Store

- **Channel class:** Template, Theme and No-Code Asset Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://themes.shopify.com/
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

Shopify’s curated marketplace for ecommerce storefront themes. Approved theme developers can sell reusable commercial designs to merchants who want professionally built Shopify storefronts.
