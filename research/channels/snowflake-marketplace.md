- **Channel class:** Data Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://app.snowflake.com/marketplace

## Description

Snowflake’s marketplace for data, applications and other data-cloud products. Providers can make commercial data products discoverable to Snowflake customers and deliver them through native sharing rather than conventional file distribution.
