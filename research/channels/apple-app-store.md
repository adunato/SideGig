# Apple App Store

- **Channel class:** Standalone Application Stores
- **Channel URL:** https://developer.apple.com/app-store/
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

Apple’s first-party application marketplace for iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, Apple TV and Vision Pro. Developers can distribute paid or free standalone applications and use Apple’s in-app purchase and subscription infrastructure to reach Apple device users.
