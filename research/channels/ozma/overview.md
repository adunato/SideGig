# Ozma

- **Channel class:** API and Microservice Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://ozma.app/
- **Assessment status:** Assessed
- **Assessment date:** 2026-08-31

## Description

Ozma is a very early-stage agent-oriented API marketplace operated by Solo Dolo Labs. It combines API discovery, a common gateway, MCP/CLI access, usage billing and Stripe Connect payouts, with a stated emphasis on API consumption by software agents as well as developers.

## Channel Assessment

Assessment framework: [../channel-assessment-framework.md](../channel-assessment-framework.md)

A higher score always means a more attractive channel for this project.

| Metric | Score (1–5) | Confidence | Evidence and rationale |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Paying demand | **1** | High | Ozma unusually publishes live production metrics. A July 30, 2026 marketplace snapshot showed **95 verified APIs, 449 all-time calls, 208 calls in the previous 24 hours and only $0.03 of 24-hour GMV**. A more recent homepage snapshot lists 113 APIs, but the visible catalogue remains dominated by free endpoints with very small call counts. This is direct evidence that current paid demand is negligible. |
| Opportunity density | **1** | High | Supply is small, but current commercial demand is smaller still. With around one hundred APIs and near-zero reported GMV, there is no evidence that the marketplace currently contains enough paid demand to support even modest seller revenue. Low competition does not compensate for the lack of buyers. |
| New-entrant attainability | **2** | Medium | Ozma actively invites providers and its small catalogue means a new API can obtain visibility. However, attainability is defined as the ability to capture meaningful demand, not merely to obtain a listing. No evidence was found of a new independent provider acquiring a material paid customer base, while live GMV indicates very little demand exists to capture. |
| Revenue potential | **1** | High | Ozma's standard 90/10 provider/platform split is attractive, but current marketplace economics are far too small. At $0.03 of reported daily GMV, the observed channel is orders of magnitude below what would be required to support the project's £2,000–£5,000 annual target. The score reflects current market reality rather than theoretical future upside. |
| Competitive pressure | **5** | High | Only roughly one hundred APIs are currently visible and many are imported public/free APIs. Direct commercial competition is therefore very low compared with mature marketplaces. This is a genuine positive metric, but the framework correctly keeps it separate from paying demand: a channel can have almost no competition and still be commercially unattractive. |
| Production leverage | **5** | High | Ozma is explicitly designed for programmatic APIs, gateways, MCP and agent consumption. Software-development and AI-assisted production capability translate directly into products suitable for the channel, and the product surface is narrow enough to automate publication and iteration. |
| Operating burden | **3** | Low | Ozma states that it handles gateway distribution, usage billing and Stripe Connect payouts, and its standard take is 10%. Public documentation is less mature than the larger marketplaces, and provider hosting/support obligations are not documented clearly enough to justify a higher score. The likely model still requires the provider to operate the underlying API continuously. |

### Supporting assessments

- **Trend:** Growing from a very small base
- **Evidence confidence:** High for demand; Medium overall

Ozma is expanding its catalogue and is technically active, but the live marketplace metrics are decisive: it is currently an experimental/early channel rather than an established source of seller revenue. This assessment illustrates why trend and opportunity must remain separate—a channel can be growing and still have negligible present commercial value.
