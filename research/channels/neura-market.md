# Neura.Market

- **Channel class:** Automation and Integration Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://neura.market/
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

A marketplace created for reusable automation workflows across n8n, Zapier, Make, Activepieces and Pipedream. It is intended to connect workflow creators with buyers who prefer purchasing tested automations rather than building them from scratch.
