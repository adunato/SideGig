# Atlassian Marketplace

- **Channel class:** Platform App, Plugin and Extension Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://marketplace.atlassian.com/
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

Atlassian’s marketplace for apps that extend Jira, Confluence and other Atlassian products. Third-party developers can sell cloud and data-center apps to business customers already paying for Atlassian software, including subscription-priced utilities and workflow extensions.
