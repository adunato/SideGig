- **Channel class:** AI Model, Agent and Inference Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://mcpize.com/

## Description

A marketplace and hosting platform for Model Context Protocol servers. Developers can publish or connect hosted MCP servers, configure subscription or usage pricing and receive marketplace revenue while MCPize handles billing and related infrastructure.
