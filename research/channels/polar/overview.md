# Polar

- **Channel class:** Direct Software and Digital-Product Storefronts
- **Channel URL:** https://polar.sh/

## Description

A developer-focused merchant-of-record and billing platform for software, subscriptions and digital products. Polar provides APIs, checkout, product management, tax compliance and automated delivery features aimed particularly at small technical teams and independent developers.
