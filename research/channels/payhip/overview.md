# Payhip

- **Channel class:** Direct Software and Digital-Product Storefronts
- **Channel URL:** https://payhip.com/
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

A lightweight storefront platform for digital downloads, software, courses and memberships. Sellers create their own store and checkout, with Payhip handling product delivery and recurring payments while demand generation remains primarily seller-led.
