- **Channel class:** Data Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://marketplace.databricks.com/

## Description

Databricks’ exchange for datasets, notebooks, AI models, applications and MCP servers. Approved providers can publish free or commercial data products into the Databricks ecosystem and reach data-science and analytics buyers.
