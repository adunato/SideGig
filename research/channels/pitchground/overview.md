# PitchGround

- **Channel class:** Software Deal and Lifetime-Licence Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://pitchground.com/

## Description

A SaaS deal marketplace built around product launches and lifetime offers. Software vendors can use PitchGround to acquire a concentrated cohort of early customers, feedback and upfront revenue through a structured marketplace campaign.
