# Ko-fi Shop

- **Channel class:** General Digital-Product Marketplaces
- **Channel URL:** https://ko-fi.com/
- **Assessment status:** Discovered

## Description

A creator commerce platform that combines supporter payments with shops, memberships and digital-product delivery. Sellers can offer downloadable products and recurring memberships to Ko-fi users without operating a separate ecommerce stack.
